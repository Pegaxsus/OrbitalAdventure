# Prompts de los cuatro ambientes · 0.1.13

Generados mediante la herramienta integrada imagegen, como PNG con transparencia. Referencias de estilo: los ambientes actuales del proyecto; generación nueva, sin editar las imágenes existentes.

## Aurora solar

Borrador inicial: assets/backgrounds/drafts/solar-aurora.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a SINGLE transparent PNG deep-space ambience layer for Hangar Orbital, a top-down 2D space game with detailed pixel-art ships. Primary request: A graceful crescent of faint champagne-gold and warm amber solar corona filaments, tiny ivory plasma wisps, no sun disk, asymmetrical open arc towards the lower-left; most of the scene empty and transparent. Style: subtle hand-painted space matte with slightly grainy pixel texture, softly layered diffuse light, matching a dark game background; quiet atmospheric far-away scenery, never a foreground object. Composition: wide horizontal landscape 16:9, all gas/light fully contained inside the canvas, smoothly dissipating to true alpha transparency well before EVERY outer edge, a transparent safety margin on all four sides. No opaque background, NO stars, NO black rectangle, no checkerboard pattern, no border, no text, no symbols, no ships, no stations, no grid. Preserve large transparent gameplay-readable negative space. True RGBA transparency, feathered organic boundaries; no cut-off clouds. This is Aurora solar.
